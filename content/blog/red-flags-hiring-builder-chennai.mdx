## Protect Yourself Before Breaking Ground

Hiring the wrong builder in Chennai is one of the most expensive mistakes a family can make. We've seen clients come to us mid-project after their previous contractor abandoned the site, substituted materials, or inflated costs by 40-60% over the original quote.

Here are the red flags you should never ignore.

## 🚩 Red Flag #1: No Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

If a builder gives you a single lump-sum quote like "₹2,200 per sq ft all-inclusive" without a detailed, line-by-line breakdown of materials, quantities, and labor — **walk away.**

A professional firm provides a BOQ that lists:
- Exact quantity of cement (bags), steel (tonnes), sand (loads)
- Specific brands and grades for every material
- Labor rates broken down by trade (mason, carpenter, electrician, plumber)
- A clear list of what's included and what's not

## 🚩 Red Flag #2: Demands Large Upfront Payment (>20%)

Professional builders operate on **milestone-based billing** — you pay when work is completed and verified. A typical milestone structure:

1. Foundation complete → 15%
2. Plinth level → 15%
3. Roof slab → 20%
4. Brick work + plastering → 20%
5. MEP + Finishing → 20%
6. Handover → 10%

If a contractor demands 40-50% upfront before starting work, they likely have cash flow problems and may divert your money to other projects.

## 🚩 Red Flag #3: No Written Contract

"Trust me, sir" is not a legal document. A professional contract must include:
- Complete scope of work with specifications
- Timeline with milestones and completion date
- Penalty clause for delays (e.g., ₹500/day after the agreed completion date)
- Material specifications with brand names
- Warranty terms (structural + MEP)
- Dispute resolution mechanism

## 🚩 Red Flag #4: Refuses to Share Material Invoices

If your builder won't show you purchase invoices for cement, steel, and other materials, they're likely substituting cheaper alternatives while charging you for premium brands. A transparent builder shares:
- Mill test certificates for steel
- Cement brand purchase bills
- Plumbing and electrical brand invoices

## 🚩 Red Flag #5: No Structural Engineer on the Team

Many builders in Chennai rely on "experience" and standard cookie-cutter designs without involving a licensed structural engineer. This means:
- No proper load calculation for columns and beams
- No soil-specific foundation design
- No seismic compliance check (Chennai is Zone III)

**Ask to see the structural engineer's drawings and license number.** If they can't produce one, your building is being designed on guesswork.

## 🚩 Red Flag #6: Sub-Contracts Everything

Some "builders" are actually brokers. They take your contract and sub-contract the actual work to a separate team of laborers, adding a markup. Problems with this:
- Zero accountability — the sub-contractor blames the builder and vice versa
- Quality drops because the sub-contractor is squeezed on margins
- Site safety becomes nobody's responsibility

## 🚩 Red Flag #7: No Site Supervision or Progress Updates

If you have to call and chase your builder for updates, something is wrong. A professional firm provides:
- Weekly photo reports of construction progress
- Stage-wise quality inspection reports
- An open-door policy for site visits anytime

## 🚩 Red Flag #8: Skips Soil Testing

A Standard Penetration Test (SPT) costs ₹15,000-25,000. Some builders skip it to "save costs." The consequence? Your foundation is designed without knowing the soil's bearing capacity. This can lead to:
- Differential settlement (one side of the building sinks more than the other)
- Major structural cracks within 2-3 years
- Foundation failure in extreme cases

## 🚩 Red Flag #9: No RERA Registration

For any project involving multiple units or apartments, RERA registration is mandatory. Even for individual houses, working with a RERA-registered builder gives you:
- Legal recourse for delays and defects
- A 5-year defect liability period
- Transparency in project timelines and finances

## 🚩 Red Flag #10: Unrealistically Low Quotes

If one builder quotes ₹1,400/sq ft when everyone else quotes ₹2,200-2,800, ask yourself where the ₹800 difference is coming from. It's coming from:
- Cheaper, unbranded materials
- Thinner steel bars (10mm instead of 12mm)
- Fewer cement bags per cubic meter of concrete
- Underpaid, unskilled labor

**The lowest quote is almost never the best value.**
