## Why Cement Selection Matters More Than You Think

Cement is not cement. The difference between the right grade for your foundation and a generic "local brand" PPC can mean the difference between a structure that lasts 100 years and one that develops cracks in 5.

In Chennai's unique coastal environment — high humidity, salt-laden air, 1,400mm annual rainfall — cement selection is a critical engineering decision, not a procurement afterthought.

## OPC 53 Grade vs PPC: The Engineering Truth

| Property | OPC 53 Grade (IS 12269) | PPC (IS 1489) |
|---|---|---|
| Compressive Strength (28 days) | ≥ 53 MPa | ≥ 33 MPa |
| Initial Setting Time | 30 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Heat of Hydration | High | Low |
| Long-term Strength (90 days) | Plateaus | Continues to gain |
| Crack Resistance | Moderate | Excellent |
| Sulphate Resistance | Low-Moderate | High |
| Best Application | Columns, beams, slabs, foundations | Masonry, plastering, coastal foundations |

### When to Use OPC 53

OPC 53 is the workhorse for **structural concrete** — the skeleton of your building. Its rapid strength gain means:

- Faster shuttering removal (formwork can be stripped in 48-72 hours for vertical members)
- Higher early strength = fewer delays on multi-story projects
- Superior load-bearing capacity for columns and beams

We use OPC 53 for all RCC work (Reinforced Cement Concrete) — foundations, columns, beams, and roof slabs.

### When to Use PPC

PPC contains fly ash (a pozzolanic material) that reacts slowly but produces a denser, more impervious concrete over time. This makes it **ideal for:**

- Brick/block masonry mortar
- Internal and external plastering
- Waterproofing applications
- Foundations in sulphate-rich soils (common in coastal Chennai)

> **The SRT Protocol:** We never mix grades. OPC 53 goes into the structure. PPC goes into the masonry. Using the wrong grade in the wrong application is a structural compromise.

## How We Test Every Batch

No cement enters our site without verification:

1. **Manufacturing date check:** We reject any cement older than 90 days. Fresh cement = reliable hydration.
2. **Cube testing (IS 516):** We cast 150mm concrete cubes for every 50 m³ of concrete poured. Tested at 7 days (must reach ≥65% of 28-day target) and 28 days.
3. **Slump cone test (IS 1199):** We verify the water-cement ratio on site. For M25 structural concrete, we maintain a slump of 75-100mm.
4. **Storage protocol:** All cement bags are stored on raised wooden planks in covered, dry sheds. Never stacked more than 10 bags high.
