## The Approval Maze: Simplified

Before a single foundation trench is dug in Chennai or anywhere in Tamil Nadu, you need a sanctioned building plan from the appropriate authority. Getting this wrong can result in **demolition notices, denied electricity connections from TANGEDCO, and zero resale value.**

Here's the definitive guide.

## DTCP vs CMDA: What's the Difference?

| Criteria | DTCP | CMDA |
|---|---|---|
| Full Form | Directorate of Town & Country Planning | Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority |
| Jurisdiction | All of Tamil Nadu (outside CMDA limits) | Chennai Metropolitan Area (Chennai + suburbs) |
| Applies To | Residential plots, layouts, individual houses | All construction within Chennai metro limits |
| Key Function | Layout approval, plot subdivision | Building plan sanction, land-use zoning |

> **In simple terms:** If your plot is inside the Chennai Metropolitan Area (which includes places like OMR, ECR, Tambaram, Avadi, Ambattur), you likely need **CMDA** approval. If you're in Kanchipuram, Tiruvallur, or other Tamil Nadu districts outside Chennai, you need **DTCP**.

## What Documents Are Required?

### For CMDA Building Plan Approval:
1. Title deed / Sale deed of the property
2. Patta and Chitta documents
3. Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 15 years
4. FMB (Field Measurement Book) sketch from the Taluk office
5. Structural stability certificate from a licensed Structural Engineer
6. Architect's drawing set — site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections
7. Soil test report
8. NOC from Fire Department (for buildings above 18m height)

### For DTCP Layout Approval:
1. Original patta for the entire land
2. FMB and survey sketch
3. Layout plan prepared by a licensed surveyor
4. Development charges payment receipt
5. Road widening / set-back compliance certificate

## Common Mistakes That Cause Rejections

1. **Set-back violations:** Not leaving the mandatory open space (typically 1.5m on sides, 3m on front for residential). This is the #1 reason for rejection.
2. **FSI/FAR over-building:** Exceeding the Floor Space Index allowed for your zone. In CMA residential zones, the standard FSI is 1.5.
3. **Unapproved additional floors:** Building a second floor when only G+1 was sanctioned. This invites demolition orders.
4. **Missing structural engineer certificate:** CMDA mandates a licensed SE's sign-off on structural drawings for buildings above G+1.

## The Consequences of Building Without Approval

- **TANGEDCO will deny permanent electricity connection** — you'll be stuck with temporary supply
- **Chennai Metro Water may deny water/sewage connection**
- **Banks will reject home loans** against unapproved properties
- **Resale value drops by 30-50%** — buyers can't get loans on unapproved buildings
- **Municipal corporation can issue a demolition notice** at any time
