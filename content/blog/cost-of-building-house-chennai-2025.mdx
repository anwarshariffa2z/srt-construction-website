## The Real Numbers: What Does It Cost to Build in Chennai?

If you've searched online for "house construction cost in Chennai," you've probably found wildly inconsistent answers ranging from ₹1,200 to ₹4,500 per square foot. The reason for this chaos is simple: **most builders don't tell you what's included and what's not.**

At SRT Constructions, we believe in radical transparency. Here's the actual breakdown.

## Per-Square-Foot Cost Ranges (2025)

| Construction Type | Cost Range (₹/sq ft) | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| Budget Residential | ₹1,600 – ₹1,900 | Basic structure, standard MEP, economy finishes |
| Mid-Range Residential | ₹2,000 – ₹2,800 | Branded materials (Ramco, Tata Tiscon), quality interiors, CPVC plumbing |
| Premium / Luxury | ₹3,000 – ₹4,500+ | Architect-designed, imported finishes, home automation, premium fixtures |
| Commercial / Industrial | ₹1,800 – ₹3,200 | PEB structures, heavy-load floors, fire safety compliance |

> **Important:** These numbers include material, labor, and basic MEP. They do NOT include land cost, registration, interior furniture, or landscaping.

## Where Does the Money Actually Go?

For a typical 2,500 sq ft premium residential villa at ₹3,200/sq ft (total: ₹80 lakhs), here's the approximate split:

- **Foundation & Structure (35-40%):** ₹28-32 lakhs — This covers excavation, soil testing, reinforced concrete framework (columns, beams, slabs), and brick/block masonry. We use Ramco OPC 53 cement and Tata Tiscon Fe500D TMT bars exclusively.

- **MEP — Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing (15-18%):** ₹12-14.4 lakhs — Concealed Polycab FRLS wiring, Ashirvad CPVC plumbing, Legrand modular switches, earthing, and water tank.

- **Interior Finishes (20-25%):** ₹16-20 lakhs — Flooring (Kajaria vitrified or imported marble), wall putty + paint (Birla White + Asian Paints Royale), false ceiling, kitchen granite, bathroom fixtures (Kohler/Jaquar).

- **External Works (8-10%):** ₹6.4-8 lakhs — Compound wall, gate, driveway, waterproofing (Dr. Fixit), external plastering + Jotun weathershield paint.

- **Miscellaneous (5-8%):** ₹4-6.4 lakhs — DTCP/CMDA approval fees, architect fees, soil testing, rainwater harvesting, and contingency.

## The Hidden Costs Nobody Warns You About

1. **Soil stabilization:** If your plot has black cotton soil or a high water table, expect ₹2-5 lakhs extra for deep foundations or pile driving.
2. **Elevation complexity:** A double-height living room or cantilevered balcony adds 15-25% to structural costs.
3. **Approval delays:** If your DTCP approval takes 3-4 months longer than expected, labor and material inflation can add 3-5% to overall costs.
4. **Vastu modifications mid-construction:** Changing column positions after casting = demolition + rework = easily ₹1-3 lakhs wasted.
