An interior fit-out project where every material was meticulously sourced and installed. The goal was to create a serene sanctuary above the bustling city, employing a minimalist color palette with rich, tactile textures.

## Bespoke Joinery & Millwork
We focused on bespoke joinery using premium Greenply, finished with imported Italian veneers. The precision required for the seamless, handle-less cabinetry meant our carpentry teams worked to millimeter tolerances. 

## Intelligent Lighting & MEP
Accented with custom lighting fixtures and a comprehensive smart home system, the lighting design enhances the spatial finishes. 
- **Wiring**: Polycab FRLS wiring ensures absolute safety.
- **Switchgear**: Legrand modular switches were flush-mounted into the custom wall paneling for a completely uninterrupted aesthetic.

The final spatial finishes exist in perfect harmony with the exterior shell, offering the client a luxurious, turnkey living experience.
