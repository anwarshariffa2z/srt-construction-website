A massive industrial contracting project built on an aggressive 12-month fast-track timeline. We mobilized significant resources, deploying our own heavy machinery to deliver a state-of-the-art facility for a global manufacturing client.

## Geotechnical Engineering
The foundation required deep soil stabilization due to the expansive clay soil typical of the Sriperumbudur region. We executed a dense grid of driven piles, capped with a massive raft foundation to distribute the heavy dynamic loads of the industrial machinery.

## Structural Execution
We used high-grade **UltraTech OPC 53 cement** mixed at our on-site batching plant for the heavy load-bearing structural engineering. 
- Over 1,500 metric tons of primary steel were tied and poured.
- Floor flatness and levelness (FF/FL) were strictly maintained to FM2 global standards, required for high-reach forklift operations.

## Rapid Mobilization
The sheer scale of the project demanded flawless logistics. Operating 24/7 in three shifts, our project management team utilized advanced BIM modeling to resolve MEP clashes before construction began, ensuring zero rework on site. The project was handed over 3 weeks ahead of the aggressive 12-month schedule.
