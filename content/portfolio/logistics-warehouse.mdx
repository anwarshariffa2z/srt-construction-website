A massive 1 Lakh sq ft warehouse featuring pre-engineered building (PEB) steel structures. Our scope included laying ultra-flat FM2 grade concrete floors capable of withstanding heavy forklift traffic, completed 3 weeks ahead of schedule.

## Speed and Scale
By utilizing pre-engineered structural steel components, we were able to drastically reduce on-site welding and assembly time. The seamless coordination of heavy lift cranes allowed us to erect the entire structural frame in record time.
