A delicate restoration of a 1940s colonial villa, marrying modern structural reinforcements with meticulously restored Chettinad woodwork. This project required a careful balance between preservation and modernization.

## Blending Old with New
We introduced hidden steel I-beams to stabilize the aging load-bearing masonry walls. The original Madras terrace roof was carefully dismantled, waterproofed using modern APP membranes, and reconstructed using the original reclaimed timber joists.
