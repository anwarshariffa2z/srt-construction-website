A high-end automotive showroom featuring a completely column-free structural steel facade, engineered to withstand cyclonic wind loads. We utilized specialized Saint-Gobain glazing to achieve the architectural vision.

## Architectural Vision
The primary design goal was absolute transparency, allowing the high-end automobiles inside to be the true stars. We engineered a massive cantilever roof and column-free spans using premium Tata Tiscon structural steel.
