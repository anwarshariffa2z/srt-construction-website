A multi-story commercial hub blending glass and steel into a timeless aesthetic. The challenge here was executing deep basement excavation in a high-density urban zone without disrupting neighboring structures. 

## Deep Excavation & Shoring
Our architecture and construction divisions worked seamlessly to eliminate standard industry friction. We implemented advanced secant pile walls and contiguous bored piles to hold back the surrounding earth during the 3-level basement excavation. Ground water table management was handled with continuous automated dewatering systems.

## The Glass Facade
The striking exterior features a high-performance unitized structural glazing system. We utilized Double Glazed Units (DGU) with a low-E coating to significantly reduce the solar heat gain coefficient (SHGC), cutting the building's HVAC load by 22% compared to standard glass.
