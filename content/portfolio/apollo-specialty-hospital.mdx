Building a hospital requires absolute precision in MEP and sanitation. We strictly utilized V-Guard and Supreme CPVC pipes for critical medical gas and water lines. The sterile environment was finished with specialized anti-bacterial epoxy flooring and hygienic wall cladding.

## Clinical Precision
Every square foot of the hospital was constructed to meet international healthcare standards. Our MEP engineering team ensured redundant power systems and hospital-grade HVAC filtration to maintain safe and sterile surgical theaters.
