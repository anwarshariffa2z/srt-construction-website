A Grade-A IT Park featuring LEED Platinum certified sustainable materials and smart HVAC systems, constructed over an aggressive 18-month timeline. The project required seamless coordination among mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering teams.

## Sustainable Infrastructure
Designed for the future, the Silicon Valley Tech Park features extensive solar panel integrations, greywater recycling, and high-efficiency low-E glass that minimizes cooling costs while maximizing natural light for thousands of tech workers.
