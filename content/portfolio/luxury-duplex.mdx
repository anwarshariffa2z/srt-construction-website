A sea-facing duplex that demanded robust weatherproofing. We employed advanced waterproofing compounds mixed with Dalmia DSP cement to protect against salt-laden coastal winds, while large floor-to-ceiling UPVC windows maximize the ocean views.

## Coastal Resilience
Building on the East Coast Road requires specialized materials that resist corrosion. We used epoxy-coated TMT bars for the reinforced concrete and marine-grade plywood for all custom interior millwork to ensure decades of structural integrity.
