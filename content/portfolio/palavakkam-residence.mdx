A stunning architectural marvel focusing on natural light and structural integrity. The client requested a blend of modern minimalism and traditional Vastu compliance, resulting in a sanctuary that breathes harmoniously with its coastal surroundings.

## Structural Excellence
We utilized **Tata Tiscon Fe500D steel** for the core framework to ensure maximum ductility and seismic resilience. Given the aggressive coastal climate of Chennai, achieving absolute structural durability was our primary concern. The foundation was engineered with deep micro-piling, stabilized against the sandy soil profile common to the Palavakkam coast.

## Coastal Weatherproofing
Protecting the aesthetics against salt-laden air required a specialized approach.
- **Exterior Finish**: We utilized weather-resistant Jotun premium paints, applying a specialized primer coat to prevent efflorescence.
- **Waterproofing**: The terrace and all sunken slabs were treated with a multi-layer Dr. Fixit APP modified bitumen membrane. A 48-hour ponding test was conducted before laying the final protective screed.

## Interior Flow & Vastu
The spatial arrangement strictly adheres to Vastu Shastra principles without compromising the modernist vision. 
Large, floor-to-ceiling UPVC windows were installed facing the East and North to capture the morning sun, while deep overhangs on the West facade prevent harsh afternoon heat penetration, naturally regulating the indoor temperature.
